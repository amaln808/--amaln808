What is Zuwardah?

A smooth paste of dates and peanut butter, made at our workshop in Bousher.

  • IngredientsDates, peanut butter.
  • Sizes250 g and 500 g jars.
  • StorageKeep in a cool, dry place and close tightly after opening.
  • AllergensContains peanuts.

By the spoon

A spoonful mid-day instead of sweets.

On bread

On toast or flatbread at breakfast.

Before training

A small snack that is easy to carry to the gym.

With oats and yoghurt

Stir it into oats, yoghurt or a smoothie.

Every jar carries its batch number

We make Zuwardah in batches and record the ingredients and production date of each one. Every jar has a label with its batch number, production date and expiry date, so each jar can be traced back to where it came from.
